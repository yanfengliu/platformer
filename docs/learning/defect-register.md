## EW-P1 — Smooth interface and rendering weakened the pixel-art style

**Reported symptom:** “Need more pixel art style”.

**Investigation:** On f9039a61268acda2cbcba47c2d6a22f630376c5b the Canvas used integer polygon vertices but browser vector fill still antialiased diagonal edges, and smooth sky/haze/vignette gradients and layered translucent lighting added continuous tones. The DOM title and brand used Georgia serif, and interface styling included smooth gradients, blurred glows and rounded/tilted details. Baseline real-key title/gameplay was inspected at 1280×800,720×560,320×640; 90 actual gameplay RAF intervals measured median/p95 16.70ms with no browser errors.

**Repair:** Authored polygons use clipped integer scanline spans coalesced into one Canvas fill per shape. Palette bands, dithered joins, block lighting, stone bevels/chips/moss and sprite accents replace smooth presentation without changing game coordinates or poses. UI uses an original local5×7 font with800-unit em/100-unit cells and CSS sizes in multiples of8px, flat colors, stepped frames/buttons/keys and segmented health. The font is23,684bytes, copied into builds and served as font/ttf; its dependency-free generator stays in the repo.

**Whole-class gate and red/green proof:** `tests/render-pixels.test.js` exercises24 production scenes with all actors, facings, three stage colors, three palettes, windup/roll and reduced effects. It rejects sloped Canvas edges, fractional transforms/rectangles, continuous gradients and over15,000 draw commands. Two helper checks compare an explicit triangle pixel mask and giant mirrored clipping/coalescing. Replaying the production gate with `EMBERWAKE_PIXEL_SOURCE` pointing to `git show f9039a6:src/render.js` fails on continuous gradient/sloped edges (one of three tests fails; the two new helper checks pass). The adapted motion gate still fails15/19 on c956015, including all seven frozen weapon paths and four windups, preserving the old defect discriminator. `tests/pixel-font.test.js` independently inspects the retained sfnt tables/checksum, English glyph coverage/nonempty contours and source consistency. Final check, all59tests and build pass.

**Browser evidence and bounds:** Actual controls, font loading, title actions, tap/held sword stages, roll, combat, mute/pause and reduced motion pass at1280×800,720×560,320×640. Sixteen native actor/facing strips were individually inspected through ready/contact/return, plus three floor themes and affected menus. Real input reached a relic after5kills, keyboard and mouse selection worked, compact Tab navigation exposed all3 cards, and wheel scrolling reached Back/Retry. Actual death/retry restored100health/floor1/zero kills. Ninety desktop gameplay RAF intervals measured median/p9516.70ms and max16.80ms, matching baseline; this is not a universal performance claim. Production state was never set. Exact revisions, digest provenance, geometry/font bounds and authored reviews are retained in `docs/work/3_pixel-style/reviews`. Local acceptance elapsed about56minutes; model calls/compute were not separately measured. Owned processes were closed/verified gone; raw evidence is removed at closure.

## EW-C1 — Repeated sword strikes had no readable combo feedback

**Reported symptom:** “Also I don't see any combo effect when I use the sword repeatedly”.

**Investigation and root cause:** On d65af01123c3e50462366ab5a059e145b8ba4037, player combo stages already cycled through three poses and damage values, but slash size/palette and sound were shared, attack events had no rendered effect, and the visible CHAIN counter measured enemy kills. A second input problem dropped an early repeated tap because the 0.17-second buffer expired before the current 0.33–0.44-second recovery finished. A real-key seed-0 probe tapped J twice 70ms apart and observed exactly one swing after 600ms, zero kills and a hidden kill-chain counter. Holding J for 1300ms then produced stages 1,2,3,1 with no browser errors; this establishes the missing feedback separately from the dropped input.

**Repair:** Each stage has actor-local numbered feedback, distinct slash color, and distinct synthesized attack sound. The third stage has a broader amber slash, FINISH label and additional attack/impact sparks. Explicit event `stage` metadata preserves damage `amount`; the separate kill-chain counter and damage/recovery timings remain intact. A single follow-up tap survives remaining recovery. Roll and damage reset the sequence, and the input clear callback removes queued actions and hit-stop edges without changing an active paused weapon pose.

**Whole-class gate and red/green proof:** `tests/sword-combo.test.js` runs production core, renderer, input and audio modules. Twelve checks cover released taps 50ms into all three recoveries at normal/faster speed, one-slot saturation, held cycles with original timings, expiry, actual projectile damage, roll, external input cancellation and hit-stop edges, event stage/damage semantics, actual Canvas stage/pip/slash drawings, reduced effects, finisher sparks and stage audio with unlock/mute/pause guards. Replaying the gate with `EMBERWAKE_COMBO_SOURCE` pointing to an ignored directory containing `git show d65af011:src/{game,render,input,audio}.js` plus `world.js` fails all twelve; repaired modules pass all twelve and the complete suite passes 55. Initial fixture omissions and helper syntax errors were corrected before the valid proof and are not counted as product failures.

**Evidence and bounds:** Automated checks are bounded to seed 1234, 120Hz, 640×360 Canvas draw commands and mocked DOM/WebAudio; they do not certify raster or actual controls. Coordinator real-key seed-0 acceptance proved the 70ms tap repair, held stages in both facings, no phantom strike after pause, distinct real audio schedules and muted silence, and actual stage-three damage in normal/reduced motion. Six native strike crops plus title/gameplay at 1280×800, 720×560 and 320×640 were inspected individually. The first combat probe killed the walker before stage three; a corrected real-control probe primed strokes at range and observed stage-two 42→19 and stage-three 19→0 damage. Exact revision, independent clean review, render digest provenance and limitations are retained in `docs/work/2_sword-combo/reviews/0_integration.md`. Raw output is deleted at closure. ## EW-W1 — Physical weapons stayed still during attacks

**Reported symptom:** “The sword doesn't move when I strike. Fix this for all weapons on all characters, me and the enemies”.

**Investigation and root cause:** On c956015, the slash used continuous attack progress while the physical sword and arm selected one fixed attacking pose. Walker, brute and boss weapons selected fixed windup poses and returned to a fixed idle pose throughout contact; the archer used a boolean drawn bow and retained a nocked arrow after firing. The Warden also drew a melee slash during its ranged volley. Existing simulation and input checks covered combat outcomes without measuring the physical weapon drawings.

**Repair:** The renderer uses attached shoulder, elbow, hand and weapon geometry with anticipation, contact, follow-through and return poses driven by simulation attack/windup clocks. Sword combos have descending, rising and overhead paths. Walker blade, archer bow/string/draw hand, brute hammer and Warden axe move in either facing; the volley raises and thrusts the axe without a melee slash. A nocked arrow belongs only to windup because the simulation fires on entry to attack. Rounded polygon vertices retain native pixel drawing without rotating the Canvas. Gameplay timing, damage and collision code are unchanged.

**Whole-class check and bound:** `tests/render-weapons.test.js` invokes the production `createRenderer` and records its actual Canvas polygons and rectangles at 640×360. Physical weapon colors exclude slash/effect drawings. Seven actor/combo paths in both facings must move at least 3px between six attack samples (2%, 12%, 30%, 58%, 82%, 99.9%), stay within 6px of their gripping hand and return within 2px of ready. Four enemy windups develop across 5%, 45% and 95%; melee transitions join within 3px. Separate checks cover distinct combos, slash alignment, actual simulation arrow creation, volley behavior, faster timing, reduced motion, clock freeze, cancellation and read-only rendering. This gate measures drawn geometry; it does not certify visible raster pixels, artwork quality, input or viewport layout. Native visual and real-control browser acceptance remain integration-owner checks.

**Red/green proof:** Replayed the final 17-check gate with `EMBERWAKE_RENDER_SOURCE` pointing to an ignored copy of `git show c956015:src/render.js`: 15 failed and 2 passed. All seven physical weapon paths failed with “physical weapon is frozen between attack samples 0/1”; all four windups failed movement, and combo uniqueness, slash alignment, ghost nocked arrow and phantom volley slash failed independently. With the repaired production renderer, all 17 passed; the complete suite passed 41. Logs are task evidence under `output/weapon-motion/red-before.txt` and `green-after.txt` while acceptance is active; the input remains reproducible from c956015 after cleanup. The red proof uses the actual old renderer rather than a pose helper or a synthetic failed assertion.

**Attempt validity:** The first fixture probe omitted enemy `type` and exercised the walker fallback for the other enemies; it was rejected and corrected before the recorded red proof. Initial bow recoil/return poses had insufficient excursion at two sampled intervals; pose changes fixed those failures without reducing the 3px assertion. The worker changed only the renderer, focused tests and records. Implementation/check elapsed time was about 17 minutes; model calls and compute were not separately measured.

### WR-1 — Half-health damage rewound an active Warden windup

**Finding and root cause:** Independent review of integrated 89ea095 found that the renderer recalculated windup duration from current health, while the simulation chooses the slow or fast duration only when entering windup and preserves the Warden's windup on damage. A sword hit crossing half health therefore changed the drawing denominator without changing the remaining attack timer.

**Repair and timing contract:** Enemy state now records `windupDuration` when entering windup and initializes `windup` from that same value. The renderer reads the chosen duration; its fallback supports older diagnostic fixtures without metadata. The core's duration formula, damage, collision, attack transition and recovery timing are unchanged. This narrowly expands the initial renderer-only scope to simulation metadata, as authorized by the coordinator.

**Red/green proof and bound:** Two production renderer/core checks start a generated seed-0 Warden windup through the actual simulation, separately for melee and volley. After a real first-combo hit changes 180/340 health to 161/340, the rendered axe is compared with an unhit control at an identical remaining windup timer; geometry must match apart from at most 1px of existing knockback translation. The simulation must then start its next windup with duration `0.88 * 0.78`, while the current windup retains `0.88`. Both checks failed on the pre-repair 8381d8e code at “health alone must not rewind the actual rendered axe”, and both pass after repair. The complete suite now passes 43 checks; check/build/diff gates pass. Ignored logs `output/weapon-motion/red-wr1.txt` and `green-wr1.txt` remain while acceptance is active. These are bounded drawing and simulation checks; browser raster and controls remain integration-owner acceptance. Repair/check elapsed time was about 5 minutes; model calls and compute were not separately measured.
